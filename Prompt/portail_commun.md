# Portail d'invocation — Rareté COMMUNE

## Specs
- Couleur cœur émissif : `#6B7A8F`
- Couleur bordure/ombre : `#3D4654`
- Intensité d'émission : faible (~0.3)
- Particules : embers/étincelles dérivant doucement, faible densité
- Pas de rayons de dieu, pas de tremblement caméra, pas d'aberration chromatique

## Prompt (à coller)

```
A detailed 3D animated interdimensional rift/portal, floating in an
empty dark void, no characters or environment visible, subject is the
portal itself.

GEOMETRY: irregular fractal tear in space, jagged non-uniform edges like
shattered glass or a frozen lightning bolt, roughly oval vertical
silhouette approximately 1.5-2 meters tall, variable edge thickness,
organic asymmetric crack pattern (not a clean circle or geometric shape).

MATERIAL: dim grey-blue emissive core #6B7A8F, darker #3D4654 bordered
edges, low emission intensity (~0.3), subtle refractive distortion of
the void background visible through the tear, understated glow, faint
volumetric light source from within the rift.

PARTICLES: soft drifting ember/spark wisps slowly floating outward from
the edges, low particle density, gentle and unhurried motion, faint
trails of smoke.

LIGHTING: subtle rim lighting on jagged edges in dim grey-blue, no god
rays, soft ambient glow only, low-key understated lighting overall.

ANIMATION TIMELINE (8 second loop):
0:00-0:02s — a small tear quietly opens in space, crack propagates
             outward without violence
0:02-0:04s — a dark indistinct silhouette becomes faintly visible floating
             within the rift
0:04-0:06s — silhouette softly gains dim grey-blue color and detail, gentle
             particle drift increases slightly, no camera movement
0:06s-loop — rift settles into slow breathing/pulsing idle animation,
             continuous faint ember particles, awaiting interaction

CAMERA: static wide shot, no push-in, shallow depth of field (rift sharp,
void background soft blur).

STYLE: dark fantasy digital 3D render, cinematic, painterly lighting
approach similar to Slay the Spire meets Darkest Dungeon art direction,
understated and modest scale for this rarity tier specifically (this is
the most common/weakest tier — should feel unremarkable, almost easy to
overlook).

POST-PROCESSING: minimal bloom, no chromatic aberration, no screen
distortion, light volumetric fog only.
```
