# Portail d'invocation — Rareté RARE

## Specs
- Couleur cœur émissif : `#2ECC71`
- Couleur bordure/ombre : `#145A32`
- Intensité d'émission : moyenne (~0.6)
- Particules : fines veines de lumière verte se propageant depuis les bords, densité faible-moyenne
- Pas de rayons de dieu ni tremblement caméra, légère aberration chromatique optionnelle

## Prompt (à coller)

```
A detailed 3D animated interdimensional rift/portal, floating in an
empty dark void, no characters or environment visible, subject is the
portal itself.

GEOMETRY: irregular fractal tear in space, jagged non-uniform edges like
shattered glass or a frozen lightning bolt, roughly oval vertical
silhouette approximately 1.5-2 meters tall, variable edge thickness,
organic asymmetric crack pattern (not a clean circle or geometric shape).

MATERIAL: vivid emerald green emissive core #2ECC71, deep #145A32
bordered edges, medium emission intensity (~0.6), fine glowing green
veins of light spreading outward from the rift's edges across the
surrounding void, moderate refractive distortion of the void background
visible through the tear, volumetric light source from within.

PARTICLES: small embers and sparks drifting outward from the edges with
more energy than a common-tier rift, moderate particle density, faint
crystalline glints catching the light.

LIGHTING: rim lighting on jagged edges in emerald green, soft volumetric
glow emanating from the rift core, no god rays yet but noticeably
brighter and more alive than the common tier.

ANIMATION TIMELINE (8 second loop):
0:00-0:02s — a tear opens in space with a brighter crack propagation than
             common tier, faint green flash at the moment of opening
0:02-0:04s — a dark silhouette becomes visible floating within the rift,
             faint green veins already crawling across it
0:04-0:06s — silhouette gains emerald green color and detail, small
             particle burst, slight camera micro-push-in
0:06s-loop — rift settles into breathing/pulsing idle animation with
             visible green vein pulses, continuous ember particles,
             awaiting interaction

CAMERA: static wide shot with a very subtle slow push-in during reveal
phase, shallow depth of field (rift sharp, void background soft blur).

STYLE: dark fantasy digital 3D render, cinematic, painterly lighting
approach similar to Slay the Spire meets Darkest Dungeon art direction,
noticeably more vibrant and energetic than the common tier but still
restrained compared to higher rarities.

POST-PROCESSING: moderate bloom, very subtle chromatic aberration on the
particle burst only, light volumetric fog.
```
