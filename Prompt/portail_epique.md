# Portail d'invocation — Rareté ÉPIQUE

## Specs
- Couleur cœur émissif : `#9B30FF`
- Couleur bordure/ombre : `#4A235A`
- Intensité d'émission : haute (~0.85)
- Particules : débris orbitants + arcs électriques violets crépitant sur les bords, densité moyenne
- Distorsion de fond marquée, légère aberration chromatique

## Prompt (à coller)

```
A highly detailed 3D animated interdimensional rift/portal, floating in
an empty dark void, no characters or environment visible, subject is the
portal itself.

GEOMETRY: irregular fractal tear in space, jagged non-uniform edges like
shattered glass or a frozen lightning bolt, roughly oval vertical
silhouette approximately 1.5-2 meters tall, variable edge thickness,
wider and more violent tear pattern than lower rarities, organic
asymmetric crack pattern (not a clean circle or geometric shape).

MATERIAL: intense violet-purple emissive core #9B30FF, deep #4A235A
bordered edges, high emission intensity (~0.85), electric purple lightning
arcs crackling along the jagged edges intermittently, pronounced
refractive distortion of the void background visible through the tear
(more warped than lower tiers), strong volumetric light source from
within the rift.

PARTICLES: small debris chunks and rock fragments orbiting the rift edges
and occasionally being pulled toward the event horizon, moderate-to-high
particle density, crackling electric spark particles along the lightning
arcs, dust motes swirling near the tear.

LIGHTING: strong rim lighting on jagged edges in violet-purple, visible
volumetric glow pulsing outward, intermittent lightning-arc illumination
flashes, dramatic higher-contrast lighting than lower tiers.

ANIMATION TIMELINE (8 second loop):
0:00-0:02s — a violent tear rips open in space, wider and more aggressive
             than lower tiers, purple flash and crackling sound cue at
             the moment of opening
0:02-0:04s — a dark silhouette becomes visible floating within the rift,
             lightning arcs occasionally illuminating it from behind
0:04-0:06s — silhouette explodes into violet-purple color and detail,
             particle burst with debris scattering, camera slow push-in,
             subtle chromatic aberration pulse
0:06s-loop — rift settles into an energetic breathing/pulsing idle
             animation with recurring lightning arcs and orbiting debris,
             awaiting interaction

CAMERA: static wide shot with a noticeable slow push-in during reveal
phase, shallow depth of field (rift sharp, void background soft blur).

STYLE: dark fantasy digital 3D render, cinematic, painterly lighting
approach similar to Slay the Spire meets Darkest Dungeon art direction,
dramatic and powerful scale for this rarity tier, should feel dangerous
and electric.

POST-PROCESSING: strong bloom, subtle chromatic aberration on burst
moments, light screen distortion/lens warp near rift edges, moderate
volumetric fog.
```
