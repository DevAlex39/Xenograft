# Portail d'invocation — Rareté LÉGENDAIRE

## Specs
- Couleur cœur émissif : dégradé `#FFD700` → `#FFFFFF` au centre
- Couleur bordure/ombre : `#B8860B`
- Intensité d'émission : très haute, en surchauffe (~1.5, bloom qui déborde)
- Particules : explosion dorée au reveal + pluie continue d'étincelles, densité élevée
- Rayons de dieu, micro-tremblement caméra, aberration chromatique + léger flou radial

## Prompt (à coller)

```
A hyper-detailed 3D animated interdimensional rift/portal, floating in an
empty dark void, no characters or environment visible, subject is the
portal itself.

GEOMETRY: irregular fractal tear in space, jagged non-uniform edges like
shattered glass or a frozen lightning bolt, roughly oval vertical
silhouette approximately 1.5-2 meters tall, variable edge thickness,
organic asymmetric crack pattern (not a clean circle or geometric shape).

MATERIAL: emissive core gradient from #FFD700 gold to #FFFFFF white at
center, #B8860B bordered edges, very high emission intensity with
intentional bloom overdrive (~1.5), volumetric light source from within
the rift as if illuminated by another dimension, refractive distortion
of the void background visible through the tear.

PARTICLES: explosive burst of golden particle embers at reveal moment,
continuous drifting spark rain in idle state, floating fragments of
"broken reality" debris orbiting the rift edges, high particle density.

LIGHTING: rim lighting on jagged edges in gold/white, volumetric god-rays
piercing outward through the darkness, bloom post-processing pushed to
overdrive intensity, dramatic high-contrast chiaroscuro.

ANIMATION TIMELINE (8 second loop):
0:00-0:02s — sudden violent tear opens in space, crack propagates outward
0:02-0:04s — a dark indistinct silhouette becomes faintly visible floating
             within the rift
0:04-0:06s — silhouette explodes into full golden detail and color, particle
             burst, camera slow push-in, screen chromatic aberration +
             subtle radial blur pulse, micro camera shake for dramatic weight
0:06s-loop — rift stabilizes into slow breathing/pulsing idle animation,
             continuous ember particles, awaiting interaction

CAMERA: static wide shot with slow subtle push-in during reveal phase,
shallow depth of field (rift sharp, void background soft blur).

STYLE: dark fantasy digital 3D render, cinematic, painterly lighting
approach similar to Slay the Spire meets Darkest Dungeon art direction,
high production value, dramatic and awe-inspiring scale for this rarity
tier specifically (this is the rarest/most powerful tier).

POST-PROCESSING: heavy bloom, chromatic aberration on burst moment,
subtle screen distortion/lens warp near rift edges, volumetric fog.
```
